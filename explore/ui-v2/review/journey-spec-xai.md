# Journey spec review

Reviewed `JOURNEY.md` against `READINESS.md`, `CORE.md`, the five concept modules, and the ui-v1 modules `build.mjs` imports. `READINESS.md` gained a G9 line during this review. G9 names `SHELL.md` and `TEXT.md`, and neither file is in the tree, so this review holds the spec to G1 through G8 only. Nothing was built. Card heights were measured in headless Chrome at 1280x800 from the concept pages, then forced to the dock width the spec names. Plant span was computed from `acquit.mjs` through the same projection `scene.mjs` uses.

The journey is the right shape. One shell, one queue, one ledger, and concepts that do not call each other. It fails the go bar as written. The dock cannot hold the cards the spec puts in it, three beats are impossible from the keys the spec lists, and two of the six cold tasks grade the wrong outcome.

## Must change before build

1. **The dock cannot hold the cards, and the spec forbids the scroll those cards already use.** Section 1 says every card fits the dock at 1280x800 without scrolling. The dock is 464 by 688 (`JOURNEY.md:28`). Measured content height, with `max-height` removed so the number is the content and not the clip.

   The clash c1 card is 560 px wide and already scrolls at 1280x800. Its box was 556 px and its content was 630 (`concepts/clash.mjs:537`, `max-height: calc(100vh - 244px)`). Forced to 464 px it was 695 px, 7 px past the dock. c2 was 560 px at the same width, so it fits. The claimed order card was 720 px at 464 (`concepts/orders.mjs:26` sets the card at 470 and lets it scroll). The gate station was 629 px of content in a 528 px clip at its own 400 px width (`concepts/gate.mjs:337`), and 643 px when forced to the drawer's 360. The bench brief was 605 px of content in a 500 px clip (`concepts/bench.mjs:573`). The bench results grid was 412 px while results were still arriving, so the brief is the one that misses.

   G3 fails a card that leaves the viewport, and `audit.mjs` already checks `offscreen` (`audit.mjs:308`). A card that scrolls inside the dock also fails the spec's own sentence. The fix is to cut the card, not to shrink the type. c1 carries a consequence, two declarations, four resolutions, and the new Shift 5 bench row. Drop the declaration bodies into a disclosure and keep the consequence and the options. The claimed order card repeats the readbacks, the three checks, the Kvarm strip, and the bindings. The dock already has the plate. The card should be the claim, the sentence, and the checks.

2. **408 px is enough for the payout cluster and not enough for the plant the beats keep asking the camera to show.** The free rect with drawer and dock open is 408 by 688, and that arithmetic is right (`JOURNEY.md:28-32`). The payout cluster (api, retry, breaker, paypal, sqlite, and the api-to-paypal belt) spans 488 by 282 in the ui-v1 projection, azimuth 45 and elevation 30. It fits the rect at scale 0.74 with 24 px of pad. The whole Acquit plant spans 724 by 359 and fits only at 0.50, against 2.21 when `fit()` uses the full 1600x900 stage (`build.mjs:333-336`). At 0.50 the machines are about 28 px across. Chips are 10.5 px and lift to clear each other (`build.mjs:68`, `build.mjs:354-367`), so a chip is wider than the machine it names.

   Beats 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, and 11 anchor retry, paypal, sqlite, and breaker (`JOURNEY.md:44-53`). Those four fit. Beat 1 does not. The world canvas is a 1600x900 viewBox (`concepts/world.mjs:814-815`) and the five sites span 1125 by 676 once the 320 px site cards are counted (`concepts/world.mjs:826`). Fitting that into 408 px draws each site at 82 by 60. The spec hides the minimap and `.bl` when covered (`JOURNEY.md:32`), and at boot neither the drawer nor the dock is open, so say that. Beat 1 must be specified with both closed. The moment U opens the drawer, the world no longer fits, and the spec should zoom to the plant the focused row names instead of shrinking all five.

   Pins are a second overlap the spec does not mention. Orders draws witness pins as chips (`concepts/orders.mjs:569-574`). Clash draws footprint labels by searching the whole canvas for a free rect (`concepts/clash.mjs:360-369`). Bench draws a pin and a label per witness plus a plate per attempt (`concepts/bench.mjs:414-415`). All of those land in the same 408 px. With the dock open, V should list footprints in the card. Drawing six labels on the plant will fail the overlap rule in `audit.mjs:244`.

3. **Beat 3 cannot pin the second witness from the keys it lists.** The keyboard route is `M, Enter, Home, . x5, Ctrl K paypal, Enter, Shift M, Enter` (`JOURNEY.md:45`). w2 is paypal at 15:35, which is `t = -55` (`concepts/core-data.mjs:12`). Replay starts at 15:30 (`acquit.mjs:248-249`), so Home seeks to 15:30 (`replay.mjs:363`) and five presses of `.` seek five minutes forward (`replay.mjs:358-359`). That lands at 15:35, on whichever machine was selected, which is retry. `ordersPin` refuses unless `selected === w.entity` (`concepts/orders.mjs:583-586`). Ctrl K then opens the palette (`actions.mjs:143`) and Enter activates the highlighted row (`palette.mjs:101`). Nothing in the route types `paypal`. The official orders steps jump with Ctrl K and the typed word, and a first-time engineer will not invent that. Put the two Replay jumps on keys, or make Ctrl K `paypal` select paypal at 15:35 in one step. The mouse route names `PayPal · 15:35` and then `Pin must keep`, which is the path the guide already has.

4. **Beat 10 settles the wrong clash, and the key it uses does not exist.** The keyboard route is `I, Shift 1` (`JOURNEY.md:52`). Beat 9 leaves c1 open. Clash `next()` cycles to the next unsettled clash (`concepts/clash.mjs:181-185`), so I opens c2, and that part works. Shift 1 on c2 is `Serialize, radar-otel first`, which is `resolutions[0]` (`concepts/core-data.mjs:112-116`). The handoff says `settled c2 serialize`, so the route and the handoff agree. The mouse route does not. It says click the c2 pin and click Serialize, and the What column says the toast fires and c1 stays open. Clicking Serialize on the c2 card settles c2 and closes the card (`concepts/clash.mjs:176-189`). c1 was never open on this route. The toast text is also wrong. Clash says `Settled before code. Decision logged to Kvarm. No file was edited.` (`concepts/clash.mjs:188`). The spec drops the first sentence's period and the word Decision.

   The same beat is unreachable a second way. The key map says a dock card eats every plant key (`JOURNEY.md:68`). I is a card key only while a card is open (`JOURNEY.md:83`). After beat 9 the c1 card is open, so the I that should move to c2 is swallowed by the card. Clash does the opposite. Its I calls `next()` even while a card is open (`concepts/clash.mjs:427-430`). Pick one. The clash behavior is the one the beat needs.

5. **Beat 11's keys do not match its own key map, and the toast describes the wrong hold.** The route is `U, Enter, Shift 2` (`JOURNEY.md:53`). U opens the drawer with row 1 focused (`JOURNEY.md:73`). Enter in the drawer is not defined. Enter is the card's primary action (`JOURNEY.md:85`), and the drawer has no card yet. Shift 2 in the drawer opens row 2 (`JOURNEY.md:86`), which is not the breaker. So the route opens the drawer and then either does nothing or opens the wrong row.

   The row order is also not the order the beat prints. Gate sorts by class, and `harm` outranks `reversal`, `idle`, and `publish` (`concepts/gate-data.mjs:17-22`, `concepts/gate.mjs:30`). The spec's row list is breaker, c1, codex, digest (`JOURNEY.md:53`). Under that sort the first row is the breaker only if c1 is classed below harm. The spec does class c1 as undo cost grows, so row 1 is the breaker and Shift 1 would open it. Write the route as `U, Shift 1, Shift 2`, and say that the drawer keeps gate's class sort. The toast is the other miss. Advise hold on the breaker stops publication of nothing. The breaker's own advice is that Droid keeps verifying radar-otel (`concepts/gate-data.mjs:34-35`). `Only publication waits` belongs to the #184 card, where `i.kvarm` is set (`concepts/gate.mjs:113`). Using it on the breaker teaches the cold agent that a hold and a block are the same thing.

6. **Beat 12 needs two keys the spec retired, and it settles c1 twice.** The route is `Esc, I, Enter, Shift 5, Enter, Enter, Shift 3, Enter` (`JOURNEY.md:54`). After beat 11 the breaker card is open. Inside the bench, Extend is Enter only when the attempt is spent (`JOURNEY.md:88`), and bench today binds Extend to U (`concepts/bench.mjs:466`). 7. **Shift 1 means five different actions, and three of them fire on the same screen.** The key map marks Shift 1 to 5 as context (`JOURNEY.md:86`). Context that the hint names is learnable. Context the hint cannot see is not. On the gate beat the drawer and the breaker card are both open (`JOURNEY.md:53`). Shift 2 in the drawer opens row 2. Shift 2 in the card advises a hold. The focused-layer rule says the card wins (`JOURNEY.md:68`), and the beat's own route assumes the drawer wins. One of them is wrong, and a cold agent cannot tell which from the screen.

   Ctrl Z is the other silent change. The map limits it to the last settle while its toast stands (`JOURNEY.md:87`). Blueprint uses Ctrl Z with no toast and no time limit (`blueprint.mjs:551`, `DECISIONS.md:10`). A user who undoes a ghost and gets a clash settlement back, or the reverse, will not try the key again. Keep blueprint's Ctrl Z in blueprint mode and clash's Ctrl Z in live, and say so in the hint.

   F8 is a third. World toggles light and dark and skips System (`concepts/world.mjs:663`). The spec cycles System, Light, Dark (`JOURNEY.md:76`). The shell owns appearance, so the cycle is the spec's to define, but the world standalone steps press F8 twice and expect to be back where they started. `JOURNEY.md:203` says those steps must pass unchanged. A three-stop cycle fails that sentence. Keep the toggle in world standalone and let the journey cycle, or change the world steps in the same round and drop the unchanged claim.

8. **The session rename gives one session two names, and the rename is not what the seam says.** The canonical table maps `droid-radar` to Droid on bot-d2 (`JOURNEY.md:102`). `droid-radar` is already Droid on `bot`, verifying radar-otel (`acquit.mjs:155`, `build.mjs:236`). The names that differ are `droid-payouts`, `cc-42`, and `cx-17` (`concepts/core-data.mjs:58`, `concepts/world-data.mjs:97`). `cc-42` and `cx-17` are world display names. `codex-paypal` is already the floor session (`build.mjs:237`). 9. **Three numbers in the fixtures do not trace, and the boot pill is the one G4 will catch.** Beat 1 shows `local + kvarm · 2304 B out` from four ledger rows (`JOURNEY.md:43`). The four rows the spec reuses are `list_repos` 768, `get_heads` 512, `get_leases` 384, and `list_pending_reviews` 640 (`JOURNEY.md:126`). That sum is 2304, so the arithmetic holds. Gate's own pill is 1664, from 640 plus two `get_reviews` of 512 (`concepts/gate-data.mjs:153-155`, `concepts/gate.mjs:26`). The spec calls that 1664 fixed (`JOURNEY.md:100`) and then drops the two `get_reviews` from the reused list. If the journey still shows the gate log, the pill and the log disagree and `audit.mjs` fails the run on `egress-sum`. Add the two rows, or drop them from the log and from the 1664 claim.

   `Codex has waited 69 min` is not in any fixture (`JOURNEY.md:132`). Gate says Codex asked at 16:21 and has waited 9 min (`concepts/gate-data.mjs:41,125`). The floor row says 9m (`build.mjs:237`). 16:21 to 17:30 is 69 minutes, so the number is a sum someone did. Put the sum in the away fixture. A reviewer cannot trace 69 to a field.

   `EVENTS.minute` is the attempt's running total, not a clock (`concepts/bench-data.mjs:41-54`, `concepts/bench.mjs:184`). A3's extension lands at minute 19 of a 20 minute budget. The spec reads that as 16:55 (`JOURNEY.md:124`). The events themselves arrive 4900 ms and 1300 ms after Fund (`concepts/bench-data.mjs:51,54`). 10. **The cold tasks grade the wrong outcome on two of the six, and a third can pass without the work.** Task 6 asks for the plant where publication is blocked and expects `format` (`JOURNEY.md:180`). Format's condition is `Check digest needs review` (`concepts/world-data.mjs:44`). Kvarm's condition is `Review blocks publication` (`concepts/world-data.mjs:28`), and the world queue's only `blocks publication` row at boot is Review #184 on the Kvarm plant (`concepts/world-data.mjs:81`). The spec moves #184 to beat 13 (`JOURNEY.md:107`), so at `beat=world` the blocked plant is still Kvarm unless the boot condition is changed first. Change the predicate to `kvarm`, or change the boot condition and the task text together. As written, an agent that reads the screen and opens Kvarm fails the task.

    Task 2 expects `ordersState.order.state === "claimed"` three seconds after the answer (`JOURNEY.md:176`). The claim waits 600 ms of rescoring plus 1400 ms of visible evidence (`concepts/orders.mjs:936-944`), and the evidence only counts while the readbacks are fully on screen and not covered (`concepts/orders.mjs:829-834`). A dock that clips the card, which item 1 says it does, stalls the claim forever. The predicate then fails an agent who answered correctly. Assert the answer at 3 seconds and the claim on its own event.

    Task 4 accepts an advised hold or an opened review (`JOURNEY.md:178`). The task says "Decide what happens to it." Opening the card is not a decision. `gateState.held` is a map (`concepts/gate.mjs:10`), and the predicate calls `.includes` on it, which a map does not have. An agent who advises the hold and an agent who does nothing can both fail the predicate for the same reason. Assert `journeyState.gate.held` contains `breaker`, or `opened` contains it, and mean the decision.

    Task 5's expert count is 3 (`JOURNEY.md:179`). The keyboard route in beat 12 is eight keys before the bench and four inside it. Fund, Extend, Pick, Record is four actions, and Extend is a key of its own. The count of 3 cannot be right on either reading. Task 1's 10 is the only count that matches a route. Home, five presses of `.`, Ctrl K, the typed word, Enter, Shift M, Enter, P, the sentence, Enter is more than 10 even after beat 3 is fixed. Recount both from the route the spec ships, and stop the G6 run from grading against a number nobody can reproduce.

11. **`conceptActive` cannot grow a mount list without breaking the steps the spec says must pass.** `conceptActive` reads the hash once at load (`build.mjs:640-641`). Orders then checks it again itself (`concepts/orders.mjs:192-194`). The spec moves that check into `conceptActive` and makes it true for every mounted concept while `c=journey` (`JOURNEY.md:145`). Orders' steps switch the hash mid-run and expect the concept to go inactive. A `conceptActive` that stays true for a mounted id fails those steps. The spec notices this for orders (`JOURNEY.md:116`) and then requires every existing steps file to pass unchanged (`JOURNEY.md:149`). Those two sentences cannot both hold once `c=journey` mounts orders. Keep `conceptKeys` as the hash check the steps rely on, and let `conceptActive` mean mounted. The orders steps that switch hash should keep calling `conceptKeys`.

    The mount shape is the larger break. Each concept is a string of script inlined into one page (`concepts/orders.mjs` ends at `ordersInit`, and `build.mjs:666` concatenates those strings). `ordersPin`, `ordersPost`, and `ordersAnswer` close over module state and are not on `window`. Clash, gate, and bench are the same, one `*Mount` function whose handlers are closures (`concepts/clash.mjs:3`, `concepts/gate.mjs:3`, `concepts/bench.mjs:3`). Exposing `act`, `state`, `hint`, and `advance` means pulling those closures out. That is a rewrite of each key handler, not a wrapper. The timers are the hard part. Orders advances on `requestAnimationFrame` and only while the readbacks are visible (`concepts/orders.mjs:927-946`). Bench advances on `setTimeout` (`concepts/bench.mjs:116`). `advance(ms)` has to drive both, or a `beat=` seed will not match the keyboard snapshot the seed pass compares against (`JOURNEY.md:168`).

    `fitRect` is smaller and still not free. `fit()` scales the 1600x900 world into the stage (`build.mjs:333-336`). The camera the concepts read is `view`, and `select()` recenters on the inspector (`build.mjs:506-507`). A free rect that excludes the dock has to change `select`, `flyTo`, and every concept's `anchor()`, because clash and gate compute screen position from `world.getBoundingClientRect()` and a fixed `W` (`concepts/clash.mjs:155-162`, `concepts/gate.mjs:15-22`). One `fitRect` on the shell does not move those.

## Should change

The beat 6 handoff says `w3 pending` (`JOURNEY.md:48`). w3's witness value is `pending` (`concepts/core-data.mjs:29`), so the word is in the fixture. The handoff reads as an order state. `ordersState.order.state` after the answer is `open`, then `claimed`. Write `w3 value pending` so the steps file does not assert the wrong field.

Beat 13 opens #184 with Shift 4 (`JOURNEY.md:55`). Shift 4 opens row 4. The queue at that point is breaker (held), codex, digest, and #184, so row 4 is #184 only if c1 and c2 have left and nothing else has arrived. The away brief then adds rows (`JOURNEY.md:131-135`). A route that counts rows breaks the moment the queue gains one. Open #184 by name from the palette, or give the row a stable key and bind I to the next publication row.

The allowance header `9 to 18 min waiting` (`JOURNEY.md:53`) is a sum across breaker 4 to 8, c1 3 to 5, and codex 1 to 2 (`concepts/gate-data.mjs:31,40`, `JOURNEY.md:125`). Digest is 1 to 3 and is in the same row list, which would make 10 to 21. c2 is 3 to 5 and is also in the queue by beat 8. The header has to name the rows it sums, and it has to be recomputed when rows arrive. A fixed `9 to 18` fails G4 the moment c2 lands.

World's F8 and the journey's F8 store appearance in different places. World reads `theme` from the hash (`concepts/world.mjs:96`). The spec stores the choice in `sessionStorage` (`JOURNEY.md:18`). A window that opens the world standalone and then the journey will not carry the choice across. One store, named in both.

The spec retires bench P and says Esc closes the bench (`JOURNEY.md:88`). Bench P toggles the card (`concepts/bench.mjs:464`). Esc closes it only when it is open (`concepts/bench.mjs:441-445`). A user who closes the bench and wants it back has no key. Shift 5 is on the c1 card, which is gone once the bench recorded. Keep P as the bench toggle. It is the one bench key a user has already learned.

`drive.mjs` hardcodes 1600x900 in two places, the window flag and the device metrics (`drive.mjs:19`, `drive.mjs:51`). `SIZE` has to replace both. Replacing one leaves the screenshot at 1600 and the layout at 1280, and every overlap check passes against the wrong viewport.

The reload in the steps outline (`JOURNEY.md:166`) is the one reload G1 forbids. The spec puts it outside the journey under test, and the end check counts navigations (`JOURNEY.md:169`). `performance.getEntriesByType("navigation")` counts the reload. The assertion as written fails the run that contains it. Assert one navigation per pass, and run the reload in a separate file.

## What I would do differently

I would not start by exposing `mount`, `unmount`, `act`, `state`, `hint`, and `advance` on all five concepts. Four of them are closures inside a generated script, and the fifth, world, owns its own canvas, its own history, and its own top bar (`concepts/world.mjs:76-112`, `concepts/world.mjs:622-632`). A uniform interface hides the one concept that is not like the others. I would let the journey own the shell, the queue, the ledger, and the dock, and have each concept export only the three things the journey calls. Those are the card HTML for the current state, the verb the focused key should run, and a snapshot for the seed pass. Timers stay inside the concept. The journey asks for a snapshot. It does not step the clock.

I would also drop the rule that a card fits without scrolling. The measured cards are 560 to 720 px against a 688 px dock, and the content that makes them tall is the content G4 wants on screen, which is the source, the witness, and the bytes. A dock that scrolls its own card, with the action row pinned to the bottom, passes G3 and keeps the evidence. The sentence to delete is the one that forbids scrolling. The sentence to keep is the one that forbids a card leaving the viewport.

The 408 px free rect should not be a layout target. It is what is left after two panels the user opened. The beats that need the plant should close the drawer first, and the beats that need the queue should close the card. The spec already has one focused layer (`JOURNEY.md:68`). Use it. A beat never needs both open, and the layout problem goes away.

I would not retoken every hard-coded color in the same round as the join. Open question 3 is right that light for the shell only fails G2 (`JOURNEY.md:189`). It is also the round that already has to rewrite five key handlers. Shipping the join in dark, with the token sheet in place and light as the next round, gets G1 green without betting the contrast audit on a sweep through ui-v1. G2 can stay amber for light until that sweep, which is what `READINESS.md` already says it is.
